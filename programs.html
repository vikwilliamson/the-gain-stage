Our Programs

Our programs are shaped by what the community tells us it needs. They're practical, participatory, and open to everyone — whether you're brand new or bringing skills you've had for years and want to pass on.

Community Workshops

Hands-on, skills-based workshops where you learn by doing. Topics range from instrument basics and repair, to audio engineering, event production, and more. No experience required — and if you've got experience, come teach.

Instrument & Gear Library

Gear shouldn't be a barrier. We're building shared access to instruments and equipment so that cost doesn't determine who gets to participate.

Hands-On Training

Experiential learning across the full range of roles that make music and creative events happen. On stage, off stage, behind the board, and everywhere in between.

Volunteer Development

Volunteering at The Gain Stage isn't just showing up — it's a pathway. We offer real opportunities to build skills, take on leadership, and grow within the organization and the broader creative community.

Arts & Music Grants

We track and share funding opportunities so that individuals and organizations in our community don't miss out just because they didn't know what was available.

Come say hello!

We're based in the Tri-Cities area of Washington State. Reach out anytime — whether you have a question, a skill to offer, or just want to see what we're about.
